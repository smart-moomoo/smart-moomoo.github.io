Santa Clara, California

My interests span AI, SW + HW (compiler, system). My research includes ApproxMLIR, an accuracy-aware compiler for compound AI systems, and Intelligent Triage, which uses LLM-extracted reasoning to make cloud incident triage interpretable. I also contributed to ByteFS, co-designing file-system and SSD firmware support for CXL-based memory-semantic storage. I’ve also trained agent harnesses for software infrastructure development, helping land contributions to LLVM and IREE in a systematic SoL way.
